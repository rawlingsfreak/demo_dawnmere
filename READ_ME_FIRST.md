# Dawnmere — before you play

Thanks for doing this. It is a demo, not a finished game, and the most useful thing you can give
back is where you got stuck. Please don't be kind about it.

---

## Opening it

**Mac** — unzip, then **right-click `Dawnmere` → Open** (double-clicking will refuse). If macOS
still blocks it: System Settings → Privacy & Security → scroll down → **Open Anyway**. It is
unsigned, which is why it does that; it is not a warning about the game.

**Windows** — unzip, and keep **`Dawnmere.exe` and `Dawnmere.pck` in the same folder**. Separate
them and it will not start. Windows will say "Windows protected your PC" → **More info** → **Run
anyway**.

It saves by itself — three slots, when you sleep, when you go through a door, and when you quit.
Escape opens the menu if you want sound off or full screen.

## It ends on purpose

When you reach the end you will see a card that says **"Dawnmere — The demo ends here."** That is
the ending, not a crash. Everything after that is still being built.

## Already known — please don't spend notes on these

- **The town is laid out badly.** Buildings sit in the wrong places and people stand where they
  were dropped. It is being redone.
- **Townsfolk don't walk.** They have their own faces now but no walking animation, so they stand
  still while they talk to you.
- **Wren doesn't match everyone else.** Her art is drawn in different proportions from the Keeper.
- **You can't rebind the keys yet.**

Everything else is fair game.

## What would actually help

Write down the **moment**, not the fix. "I stood in the square for a minute not knowing where to
go" is worth more than "you should add an arrow."

### Austin — you have not read any of this before

That is the point. You are the only person who can answer these.

1. **Where did you not know what to do next?** Any moment at all, even five seconds of it.
2. **What did you press that didn't work?** Anything you expected to do something and it didn't.
3. **Did you ever not know where to go?** Where, and for how long.
4. **When did you first understand what this game is about?** If you never quite did, say that —
   that is the most useful answer on this page.
5. **Where were you bored?** Be blunt. Walking, reading, fighting, anything.
6. **Did fighting make sense?** Nobody explains it to you on purpose. Did that work, or did you
   just mash?

### Kaylyn — you have read the quests but never played them

You are the only person who can compare the two.

1. **Where does the game fail to say something you know from the documents?** This is the big one.
   You know things the game may never actually tell a player. Every time you caught yourself
   filling in a gap from memory, write it down.
2. **Who did you recognise, and who was just a name?** Rowan, Mara, Thom, Nessa, Wren.
3. **Did the Keeper feel like the person in the quest book?**
4. **Did anything land differently played than read?** Better or worse.
5. **At the end, when the game asks you to decide something** — did you feel you had enough to
   decide with?

## Sending it back

Anything is fine: a text, a list, a voice memo while you play. "After I talked to the old man by
the fountain" is a precise enough timestamp — no need for anything formal.

If it crashes, say what you were doing right before. That is usually enough to find it.
